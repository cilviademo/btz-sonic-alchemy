# Wes Anderson → BTZ Plugin Design Translation

## Core Wes Anderson Principles:
1. **Perfect symmetry** — everything centered, balanced, mirror-image compositions
2. **Pastel warmth** — muted, desaturated warm tones (mustard, dusty rose, sage, powder blue)
3. **Retro typography** — serif fonts, wide letter-spacing, all-caps labels, vintage typewriter feel
4. **Deliberate borders** — thin precise lines framing everything, like a diorama or dollhouse
5. **Flat color blocks** — minimal gradients, solid matte color fills
6. **Vintage analog feel** — like a 1960s/70s piece of equipment
7. **Whimsy in restraint** — playful but controlled, never chaotic
8. **Centered compositions** — dead-center alignment, mathematical precision
9. **Muted contrast** — nothing screams, everything whispers elegantly
10. **Ornamental details** — small decorative flourishes, thin rules, corner embellishments

## BTZ Palette (Wes Anderson "Fantastic Mr. Fox" meets "Grand Budapest"):
- Background: Warm parchment/cream (#F4EDE4) — like aged paper
- Panel: Dusty mustard/gold (#E8C87A) — warm, inviting, vintage
- Well/Inset: Muted sage (#A8B5A0) — the green from Moonrise Kingdom
- Primary accent: Burnt sienna/rust (#C17A4A) — warm orange-brown, like Mr. Fox
- Secondary accent: Dusty teal (#6B9B9E) — muted blue-green, Life Aquatic
- Tertiary: Dusty rose (#C4887A) — warm pink-brown for warnings
- Text: Deep brown-black (#2C2418) — warm, not pure black
- Borders: Thin, precise, warm brown (#8B7355)

## Typography (Wes Anderson):
- Display: "Futura" or "Playfair Display" — geometric or elegant serif
- Labels: ALL CAPS with WIDE letter-spacing (0.3em+)
- Values: Monospace with vintage feel — "Courier" or "IBM Plex Mono"
- Thin decorative rules above/below section headers

## Layout Principles:
- Dead-center symmetry in all views
- Thin precise borders around every panel (1px warm brown)
- Generous padding — things feel "placed" not "packed"
- Knobs arranged in perfect geometric grids
- Section headers with thin rules above and below (like chapter titles)
- Rounded corners very subtle (2-4px) — not modern pill shapes
