# BTZ Sonic Alchemy — Product Focus & Identity

## 1. Product Identity Audit

**What does BTZ actually DO in one sentence?**
BTZ Sonic Alchemy intelligently shapes, glues, and saturates audio to hit exact loudness and spectral targets without destroying dynamic range.

**What is the single strongest differentiator?**
The **Target Lock Engine**. While other plugins offer auto-gain, BTZ allows users to type in a specific LUFS, RMS, or per-band spectral target, and the plugin transparently drives the saturation and dynamics engines to hit that exact number while preserving a user-defined dynamic range threshold.

**What feature should become the flagship identity?**
**Target Lock**. It solves the biggest problem in modern mixing/mastering: "How do I get this loud and thick without crushing it?"

**Which systems support the flagship?**
- The 9-model saturation engine (provides the harmonic density needed to hit targets musically).
- The TruePeak Limiter (catches the strays).
- The BS.1770 Loudness Meter (provides the measurement truth).

**Which systems dilute the identity?**
- The LFOs. While cool for sound design, they distract from the core "intelligent mixing/mastering" identity.
- The Mid/Side encoder. It's useful, but generic.

**Feature Perception Matrix:**
- **Premium:** Target Lock, Neural Saturation Models, Ivory System UI, Harmonic Visualizer.
- **Gimmicky:** LFOs on a mastering/bus processor.
- **Unfinished:** Neural slots (need actual weights), TruePeak (needs 4x ISP verification).
- **Redundant:** Standard Mode (Simple is for beginners, Advanced is for pros; Standard sits in an awkward middle ground).
- **Hidden Gems:** The Delta monitoring mode (hearing exactly what harmonics are being added).

## 2. Positioning & Taglines

**Positioning Statement:**
For modern producers and mixing engineers who struggle to achieve competitive loudness without sacrificing punch, BTZ Sonic Alchemy is an intelligent saturation bus processor that locks your audio to exact LUFS and spectral targets. Unlike traditional limiters or clippers, BTZ uses neural-modeled harmonics and a dynamic threshold engine to achieve thickness and volume musically.

**Tagline Candidates:**
- *Dial in the tone. Lock in the target.*
- *Intelligent saturation. Absolute loudness control.*
- *The smartest way to get loud.*
- *Harmonic density with mathematical precision.*

| Competitor | Core Hook | How BTZ Target Lock Beats It |
|------------|-----------|------------------------------|
| **Soothe2** | Resonance suppression | Soothe removes bad; BTZ adds good (harmonics) to hit a target. |

## 4. Feature Prioritization Strategy

**Central (The Core Identity):**
- Target Lock Engine (LUFS, RMS, Spectral)
- 9-Model Saturation (Tube, Tape, Transistor, Neural)
- Delta Monitoring
- Harmonic Visualizer

**Advanced-Only (Hide from default view):**
- Multiband crossover points
- TruePeak Limiter ceiling/release tweaks
- Glue Compressor attack/release

**Defer to v1.2 / Future:**
- LFOs and Modulation routing (move to a separate "BTZ Modulator" plugin later).
- Mid/Side processing (keep it simple for v1.0).
