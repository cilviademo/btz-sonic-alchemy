BTZ Sonic Alchemy — Documentation Style Guide

This guide ensures all future BTZ documentation maintains the premium "Ivory System" aesthetic.

1. TYPOGRAPHY
- Headings (H1, H2): Inter SemiBold. Clean, modern, architectural.
- Body Text: Inter Regular. Highly readable.
- Values/Code/Parameters: JetBrains Mono. Used whenever referencing a specific parameter value (e.g., `-14 LUFS`) or code.

2. COLOR PALETTE
- Background: Ivory (#F5F0E8)
- Surface/Panels: Parchment (#EDE7DB)
- Primary Accent: Sage Green (#7A9E7E) — used for dynamics, EQ, and positive indicators.
- Secondary Accent: Terracotta (#C17B4A) — used for saturation, drive, and warnings.
- Text: Charcoal (#2D2A26) — never pure black (#000000).

3. PAGE LAYOUT
- Margins: Generous. At least 1.5 inches on all sides. Let the text breathe.
- Alignment: Left-aligned body text. Never justified (creates ugly rivers of whitespace).
- Paragraph Spacing: 1.5 line height, with 12pt space after paragraphs. No first-line indents.

4. DIAGRAM STYLE
- Diagrams should look like architectural blueprints or boutique synth manuals.
- Use thin, crisp lines (1pt or 2pt).
- Use the Sage and Terracotta colors sparingly to highlight signal flow.
- Avoid 3D effects, drop shadows, or gradients in diagrams. Keep them flat and technical.

5. TONE OF VOICE
- Confident but not arrogant.
- Educational but not academic.
- Musical. Talk about "weight," "glue," and "air," not just "Hz" and "dB."
- Avoid generic marketing fluff ("game-changing," "ultimate"). Let the features speak for themselves.
